BioCrisis

First-person survival horror: a house at night, scarce ammo and zombies that can hear you.

Play BioCrisis
Role
Game design, programming and art
Status
Playable in the browser
Tools
TypeScript, Phaser, Vite, Vitest

What it is

A custom raycasting engine, zombies that chase with A* and open doors, three weapons, an inventory and a final boss. It runs on desktop, mobile and gamepad.

What I did

  • Engine and logic in pure TypeScript, covered by tests
  • Textures, sprites, HUD and logo generated in code
  • Spatial audio and touch controls
  • A simulator that plays on its own to measure balance
