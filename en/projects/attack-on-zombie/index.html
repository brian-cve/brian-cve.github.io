Attack On Zombie

Seven nights in an abandoned office: finish an 8-bit game before six in the morning while holding off the waves.

Status
In development for Android
Tools

What it is

A top-down mobile game with one rule above all others: a stable 30 FPS on a low to mid-range phone. Every effect has a budget and is measured on a real device.

What I did

  • Game design and programming
  • Art direction: low poly, flat color, rainy night
