A Last Haul

Horror behind the wheel: one more night on the road, coffee to stay awake and something that climbs into the cab.

Role
Game design, programming, 3D art, interface and identity
Status
Coming soon
Tools
Unity 6, C#, Blender, Illustrator, Photoshop
Team
Goat Studios

What it is

You drive a truck at night down an endless road, with the look of late-nineties games. Keep an eye on sleep, speed, mirrors and the radio.

What I did

  • Level design, win and lose conditions, and game logic
  • C# programming of the steering wheel with IK-driven arms, the speedometer and the bobblehead that reacts to the truck’s inertia
  • Programming of the cab HUD (time, gears, sleep and coffee) and the main menu
  • Models, cab dressing and dashboard instruments
  • PS1-look experiments
  • Interface icons and gauges
  • Logo and cover art
