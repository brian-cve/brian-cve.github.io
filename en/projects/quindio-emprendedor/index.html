What it is

A support program for small businesses in Quindío needed its whole process in one place. The platform handles roles, PDF documents, assessments, jury scoring, signed visit records and automatic emails.

What I did

  • About 80% of the platform: registration, evaluation, pitch, visits, characterization, annexes and deliverables
  • Responsive interfaces and validated forms
  • Supabase data layer: 80+ migrations and permissions for six roles
  • Ten-stage workflow: automatic document scoring, pitch rubrics and beneficiary selection
  • Official PDF documents with digital signature, bulk regeneration and Excel exports

Names, ID numbers, contact details and figures were removed from the screens to protect participants.
