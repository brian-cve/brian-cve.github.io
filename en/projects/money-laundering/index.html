Money Laundering

One room, a washing machine that is a roulette wheel and a mountain of bills that never stops growing.

Role
3D art, environment, interface and identity
Status
Coming soon
Tools
Unity 6, URP, Blender, Illustrator
Team
Goat Studios

What it is

A first-person gambling roguelite. Every three days a bigger quota lands; each wash is a bet on red, black or green, and upgrades repaint the wheel in your favor.

What I did

  • Modeling of the roulette washer, the vending machine and the bills
  • Set dressing, lighting and shader for the room
  • Game interface, main menu and in-game computer
  • Logo and store artwork
