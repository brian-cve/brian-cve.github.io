Rubymon

A turn-based pixel-art RPG with a town to explore, battles and music composed in code.

Play Rubymon
Role
Game design and programming
Status
Practice project, playable in the browser
Tools
TypeScript, Phaser, Vite, Tiled

What it is

A technical exercise on classic handheld RPGs: Tiled maps, a combat system with types and status, party, bag and a distinct animation for every move.

What I did

  • Combat and capture engine with automated tests
  • Per-move animations and particles
  • Procedural audio with Web Audio: effects and three songs
  • Logo and HUD drawn in code
