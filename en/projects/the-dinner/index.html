What it is

The Dinner is Goat Studios’ first commercial game. The whole campaign turns on one idea: two faces under the same helmet, red and black, nothing in between.

What I did

  • Game and level design
  • C# programming: game systems, puzzles, interaction and AI behaviors
  • Lighting and cinematic sequences
  • Game identity, logo and key art
  • Launch campaign: posters, countdown, Steam capsules and press kit
  • Printed pieces for fairs: booth, T-shirts and stickers
  • Artwork adapted to the store’s eleven languages
